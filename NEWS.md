## A. THREE BIG HEADLINES (1–3)

### 1. HUAWEI GUILTY OF TRADE-SECRET THEFT AND BANK FRAUD
- Region: North America — Source: AP — Pin: New York, USA (41N 74W)
- A US jury in Brooklyn convicted Huawei of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, guilty on 10 of 12 counts, but acquitted it of racketeering, the most serious charge. Huawei said it expects to appeal, with sentencing set for September 2027.
- Illustration: a judge's gavel over a smartphone with a cracked screen, and a red "GUILTY" stamp.

### 2. FOUR TANKERS HIT MINES IN STRAIT OF HORMUZ
- Region: Middle East — Source: Al Jazeera — Pin: Strait of Hormuz (26N 56E)
- Iran's Revolutionary Guard said three oil tankers hit naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire, then reported a fourth tanker hit, bringing the total to four. The Joint Maritime Information Centre said the mine claims were unconfirmed.
- Illustration: a strait between two cliffs with three burning tankers, black smoke columns, and a small mine bobbing in the water.

### 3. HOUTHI STRIKE HITS RIYADH TOWER, 11 HURT
- Region: Middle East — Source: Reuters / AFP — Pin: Riyadh, Saudi Arabia (25N 47E)
- A Houthi attack hit a tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District, home to the Saudi Public Investment Fund, wounding at least 11 people. It was the second hit on the capital's tallest skyscraper in under a week.
- Illustration: a glass skyscraper with a smoke plume from its upper floors, a small drone above it, and ambulances at its base.

## B. TEN SMALL STORIES WITH ILLUSTRATION (4–13)

### 4. UKRAINE HITS RUSSIAN SHIPS OFF LIBYA AND FINLAND
- Region: Europe — Source: Reuters / The Guardian — Pin: Benghazi, Libya (32N 20E)
- Ukraine said naval drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury and damaged the cargo ship MV Angara off Benghazi, and acknowledged a drone strike on the cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland. Libya said the corvette was severely damaged, not sunk.
- Illustration: a warship listing and smoking beside a small drone boat, with waves and a distant port.

### 5. NVIDIA'S HUANG IN THERAPY AS SHARES FALL 7%
- Region: North America — Source: WSJ / AP — No pin (company story, Santa Clara HQ)
- A day after his reinstatement as Nvidia chief, Jensen Huang apologised to staff, said he had made terrible mistakes and was in therapy. Nvidia shares fell about 7% on Tuesday as the comeback rally faded.
- Illustration: a falling stock chart with a green chip at the top sliding down a steep red line, and a small office chair below.

### 6. CHINA PROBES POLITBURO MEMBER CHEN JINING
- Region: Asia — Source: Reuters — Pin: Shanghai, China (31N 121E)
- Shanghai party chief Chen Jining, a member of China's elite Politburo, is under investigation, state-run Xinhua said on Tuesday. He is the fourth member of the top leadership body to be probed since 2025.
- Illustration: a Shanghai skyline with a magnifying glass over a red official seal and a document stamped "UNDER INVESTIGATION".

### 7. SENEGAL REGAIN AFCON TITLE ON APPEAL
- Region: Africa — Source: Reuters — Pin: Dakar, Senegal (15N 17W)
- The Court of Arbitration for Sport on Tuesday reinstated Senegal as 2025 Africa Cup of Nations winners, upholding their appeal against CAF stripping the title. Senegal's 1-0 extra-time win over Morocco in January stands.
- Illustration: a football trophy on a podium with a judge's gavel beside it, and green-yellow-red confetti falling.

### 8. SANOFI BUYS INSMED FOR $41 BILLION
- Region: Europe — Source: WSJ / Sanofi — Pin: Paris, France (49N 2E)
- French drugmaker Sanofi agreed to buy US biotech Insmed for $41 billion in cash, $175 a share, the largest biotech deal in nearly three years. The deal adds respiratory drugs Brinsupri and Arikayce and should close in early 2027.
- Illustration: a handshake over a giant pill bottle and a $41bn price tag, with a French flag and a US flag on either side.

### 9. TURKISH HELICOPTER CRASH KILLS FIVE
- Region: Europe — Source: AP — Pin: Eskisehir, Turkiye (40N 30E)
- A Turkish Air Force AS-532 helicopter crashed after taking off from Eskisehir for training, killing all five crew members, the defence ministry said. It came 12 days after a similar crash killed four near Malatya.
- Illustration: a helicopter tilted over green hills with a search-and-rescue team and a red cross tent below.

### 10. HANOI RESTAURANT BLAST KILLS SIX
- Region: Asia — Source: AP / SCMP — Pin: Hanoi, Vietnam (21N 106E)
- An explosion at a hotpot restaurant in Hanoi's Dong Da district killed at least six people and injured at least ten, with rescuers searching the rubble for survivors. Authorities suspected a cooking gas leak.
- Illustration: a restaurant front with a burst of orange flame from its doorway, a gas cylinder tipped over, and rescue workers with a stretcher.

### 11. CARTAGENA HOSTAGES: 25 HELD IN SUPERMARKET
- Region: South America — Source: AP — Pin: Cartagena, Colombia (10N 76W)
- Armed men seized a supermarket in Cartagena and held 25 people hostage, President Abelardo de la Espriella said. Five older women were released, troops and police were deployed, and the airport briefly suspended landings.
- Illustration: a supermarket front with a police cordon, a helicopter overhead, and a small group of people being led out by officers.

### 12. LANDMINE TOLL HITS FIVE-YEAR HIGH
- Region: Global — Source: AP — No pin (global report)
- Landmines and unexploded ordnance killed or injured 6,929 people in 2025, the highest toll since 2020, the Landmine Monitor 2026 report said on Tuesday. Myanmar led with 1,891 casualties, Syria followed with 1,602, and civilians were 84% of victims.
- Illustration: a deminer in a helmet and vest kneeling by a red triangular mine warning sign in a bare field.

### 13. NATO VOWS TO DEFEND SPACE AFTER NUCLEAR REPORTS
- Region: Europe — Source: Reuters / France 24 — No pin (alliance statement)
- NATO chief Mark Rutte said the alliance will defend against threats in space after German media reported Russia may launch a nuclear-armed satellite within 60 days. Moscow dismissed the reports, and Rutte spoke on Monday.
- Illustration: a satellite with a red warning triangle orbiting Earth, a NATO compass-star shield below, and a dotted orbit line.
